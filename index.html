Project Overview

About AMC Smart Complaint System

The Smart Municipal Complaint Management System is an end-to-end civic service platform built for modern municipal corporations. It bridges the gap between citizens and city maintenance departments.

Citizen Profile & Role Tagging

Allows users (Students, Employees, Business owners, Senior Citizens) to specify their role for prioritized routing.

Visual Proof & Photo Upload

Citizens can attach images of broken infrastructure or uncollected waste for instant inspection.

Dynamic Status Tracking

Track progress step-by-step: Pending → Confirmed → On Way → Resolved.
